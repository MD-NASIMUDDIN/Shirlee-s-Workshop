Meet Your Teacher

Shirlee

Welcome to Shirlee's Workshop — your creative home in Mill Hill, London. Shirlee is an artist and educator with a BA in art and over 20 years of teaching experience, running in-person classes for children, teens and adults.

Whether it's a first painting class, building a GCSE/A-Level portfolio, joining a weekly sketching club, or celebrating a birthday with a hands-on art party — every session is personal, encouraging and rooted in real technique.
